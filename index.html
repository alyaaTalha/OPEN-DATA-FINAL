Land Grants

This register documents the social housing journey at the point where eligible families apply for and are allocated residential plots.
It includes the service type requested, application and approval dates, current status (e.g., under review, approved), and high-level household attributes (e.g., family size, age group, marital status). Personally identifiable information is excluded. The dataset enables transparent tracking of demand, approval throughput, and equity in access to land grants across communities.

About this dataset

  • Approvals vs. beneficiaries by month to compare ratio of approvals processed to beneficiaries and others that has not yet.

  • Average time from application to approval to assess service responsiveness.

  • Approvals by family size to guide the right housing requirements in upcoming projects.

  • Approvals by age group or marital status to check fairness and inform outreach.

Master Constructions

This register monitors delivery once funding and contracts are in place and homes are under construction. It records project status (in progress/completed), percentage completion, planned vs. actual timelines, and headline financials (loan/contract amounts).
It provides an operational view across contractors to identify delays early, manage cost pressures, and confirm on-time, on-budget, quality delivery. It also supports handover planning and beneficiary communications.

About this dataset

  • Track % completion and flag projects past planned end dates.

  • Compare average contract amounts by year/contractor.

  • Count monthly completions to plan handovers and media updates.

Projects & Villas

This is the authoritative catalogue of MBRHE housing projects, that shows what is being built, where, and a short description of each project.
It serves as the primary index that 'Projects - Villas' dataset could link to in order to map the Location attribute. It helps partners (utilities, roads, schools, clinics) coordinate their works and allows residents and media to quickly answer, “What is happening in my area?”
